Privacy Policyسياسة الخصوصية

Last updated: 22 October 2026

In short: we do not collect, store or share any personal data. Syria Prayer ("the app") is published by Level 7 Techs. Everything it does happens on your device.

  • No accounts
  • No ads
  • No analytics
  • No tracking

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your location to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. These calculations run entirely on your device, and location is used only while the app is open, never in the background.

  • Your location is saved only on your device, so the app works offline.
  • To show the name of your city, the app asks your phone's built-in geocoding service (Apple on iOS, Google on Android) to turn your coordinates into a place name. That request is handled by the operating system under Apple's or Google's privacy policy. We never receive it.
  • You can skip GPS entirely and choose a city from the offline list.

Data stored on your device

Your settings (language, theme, calculation method, notification choices), your selected location, adhkar progress and Tasbih counts are stored locally on your device. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Notifications

Prayer reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No server or push service is involved.

Widgets and watches

Home screen widgets and the Apple Watch and Wear OS apps get the prayer schedule directly from the app on your phone, through the operating system's own channels. Nothing passes through our servers. We don't have any.

Contact us by email

If you choose Contact us in Settings, your email app opens with a pre-filled message that includes the app version, device model and language to help with support. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself. We use your email only to reply to you.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Third-party data

City names come from GeoNames (CC BY 4.0) and ship inside the app. No requests are made to GeoNames.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
